# Sessia — design.md

## Brief
Sessia is a BNB Chain tokenized-stock research agent. Its job is to make evidence, market-state, and estimated execution impact understandable before a user chooses to act.

## Reference Notes
- **BNB Chain:** short declarative headlines, high-contrast editorial layouts, metric strips, strong practical utility cards, and a sharp black-white-yellow hierarchy.
- **BNB Agent Studio:** direct sentence-pair positioning such as “Prompt in. Agent out.”; agent state is made concrete with logs, status labels, clear stages, and compact console-like surfaces.
- **Binance:** dense market snapshots, familiar exchange spacing, active state shown through restrained green, and yellow reserved for decisive actions.
- **Brand constraint:** say “Built on BNB Chain” or “Available on BNB Chain”, never imply official partnership or endorsement. Do not alter or recreate the BNB Chain logo.

## Product Feel
Decisive, evidence-led, utilitarian. A research terminal with BNB's editorial confidence, not a glossy generic DeFi dashboard.

## Audience
On-chain investors and researchers monitoring tokenized stocks.

## Accent Color
BNB yellow #F0B90B. Use it only for primary actions, status markers, selected range marker, and important numeric output. Green means an operational state, never profit.

## Typography
Space Grotesk for compact bold display, DM Mono for data and system labels. Headlines use short, punchy sentence-pairs.

## Copy Voice
Confident, terse, active. State what was observed, what was calculated, and what is unknown. Never call a signal profit, opportunity, or risk-free.

## User Flow
Landing and agent status → investigation → evidence modal → route simulation → wallet review gate.
